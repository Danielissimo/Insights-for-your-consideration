Manifesto

An Invitation

There is no shortage of opinions.

No shortage of information.

No shortage of certainty.

This project began with a different question:

How might we create a small space in which people become just a little more curious than certain?

Not persuaded.
Not instructed.
Not corrected.

Simply invited.

Each piece is called an Insight.

Not because it is the truth.
Because it is one way of looking.

What follows is not something to believe.
It is something to consider.

The Insight is mine to offer.
The Reflection is yours to have.

What This Is

Insights for Your Consideration is neither a lecture, nor spoken-word poetry, nor a concert.

It lives somewhere between them.

Each Insight is a conversation between spoken reflection, silence and saxophone.

Sometimes the words lead.
Sometimes the saxophone does.

Neither illustrates the other.
Neither accompanies the other.

Together, they point towards something neither could fully express alone.

Why It Exists

We live at a time when it has never been easier to produce answers.

The harder task is creating space for better questions.

Questions slow us down.
Questions soften certainty.
Questions make curiosity possible.

This project exists to protect that space.

Not to tell people what to think.
To invite them to think again.

The Nature of an Insight

An Insight is not advice.

It is not instruction.
It is not an argument.

It is an offering.

The creator is responsible for the quality and integrity of the Insight.

What follows belongs to the listener.

A Reflection cannot be delivered.
It can only occur.

The Insight is offered.
The Reflection remains free.

The Invitation

If these Insights leave you with one fewer certainty, one better question, one quieter breath, or one unexpected smile, then they have already become more than words.

They have become yours.

ONE.1

Start Here. Or Not.

We tell ourselves…
we’re in pursuit of understanding.

Yet often…
we settle for the illusion of certainty.

Certainty about what’s happening.
About what’s going to happen.
Above all…
about who we are.

Along the way…
our beliefs begin to take hold…
of our identity.

But what if…
certainty is familiarity mistaken for truth?

What if strength…
is holding an idea…
without letting it hold you?

Now…
imagine expressing one of your deepest convictions.

Clearly.
Articulately.
Unapologetically.

Hold it with all your heart.

Then…
leave just enough room…
for a heartbeat of doubt.

Two quiet words…
that almost smile back at us.

“…or not.”

A gentle invitation…
to leave room…
for what else might be true.

Those two little words…
make space…
for two bigger ones.

Humility.

And courage.

The humility to accept…
that you might be wrong.

The courage…
to keep exploring…
beyond what feels familiar.

After all…
we’ve all been certain about things…
that later made less sense…
than they once did.

So the next time you find yourself saying…
“This is how the world works.”

Consider…
adding two quiet words.

“…or not.”

That’s all it takes…
to soften the edges…
of your convictions.

So that new meaning…
may begin to emerge.

…or not.

ONE.3

Let There Be…

Darkness.

When darkness comes…
Be still.

Just for a moment.

Breathe.

Let your eyes adjust.

Darkness changes everything…
without changing anything.

It asks something different of us.

When sight falls away…
so do many of the choices it offers.

Not because there is less around you…
but because there is less…
pulling you away from yourself.

Something deeper…
comes into view.

Just like dancing blindfolded.

You don’t have to stop moving.
You move differently.

More quietly.
More intentionally.

When your eyes can no longer lead…
the rest of you…
begins to.

Darkness doesn’t change the world.
It changes you.

And that changes…
how you meet it.

Perhaps darkness…
is not something to overcome.

It is simply…
the absence of light.

So rather than fight the darkness…
find your way back…
to the light.

And suddenly…
darkness has nowhere to hide.

We’ve given different names…
to whatever helps us…
find our way again.

Truth.
Love.
Faith.
Awareness.

Different words…
pointing in the same direction.

The word matters less…
than the practice.

Mine happens to be…
the practice of Flow…
which allows me…
to meet the world…
a little more harmoniously.

Do you have one?

A thought.
A breath.
A movement.
A note.

When darkness comes…
Be still.

Just for a moment.

Breathe.

Smile.

Because you can hide everything…
in darkness…
except light itself.

Let there be light.

…and the reflection…
it brings.

…or not.

ONE.5

Golf:
The Tai Chi of Capitalists?

With your feet…
firmly planted on the ground…
you look down…
at the ball.

Then out…
towards where you want it to go.

Then back down…
to the ball.

And suddenly…
the distance between them…
hits you.

You tighten your grip…
ready to strike.

And yet…
the tighter you hold…
the more force you apply…
the more your swing…
loses its flow.

You want your shot…
to travel farther.

Not because…
you hit it harder…
but because strength…
finds its rhythm…
and control…
learns to let go.

Perhaps…
that same insight…
echoes…
(echoes…)
beyond the golf course.

We put so much effort
into bringing things into being…
pushing…
rushing…
powering through…

that eventually…
we search for somewhere
to slow down…
to breathe…
simply to be.

As though being and doing
belonged to different worlds.

Taoist philosophy…
brings them together.

Rooted like a tree…
yet dancing like its branches
in the wind.

Planning and playing.
Being and doing.

Different movements…
of the same dance.

And when those movements…
find harmony…
what emerges…
is called WU-WEI.

Acting…
without forcing.

Effortless action.

So whether you are
playing golf…
playing music…
or calling a new idea
into being…

breathe.

And swing.

WU-WEI…
all the way.

…or not.
