Start Here. Or Not.
Certainty

Certainty often feels like truth because it feels familiar. Wisdom begins when we can hold our deepest convictions wholeheartedly — without letting them hold us. Two quiet words leave room for what else might be true.

Call It Into Being.
Direction

A name at the beginning asks a different question than a name at the end: not "what has this become," but "what belongs here." It keeps the conversation centred. Call a name into being — and witness what, in turn, it calls into being.

Let There Be…
Perception

Darkness doesn't change the world. It changes you — and that changes how you meet it. We give different names to whatever helps us find our way back to the light. Mine happens to be the practice of Flow.

My Cat, My Dog,
Chess & Tetris
Mode of Engagement

My cat, Gigimo, played Chess — patient, deliberate, always a few moves ahead. My dog Norbert plays Tetris — meeting what comes, guiding it, until it finds its place. Some things can only be discovered by playing. So before asking "What's my next move?" — perhaps ask "What game am I playing?"
